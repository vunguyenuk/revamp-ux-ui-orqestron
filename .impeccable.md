## Design Context

### Users
Real estate agents and transaction coordinators working through dense California transaction forms under time pressure. Their primary job is to review, complete, organize, and send forms without losing their place in the active PDF.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, and quietly intelligent. Orqestron should feel trustworthy enough for compliance work while making AI assistance feel practical rather than experimental.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light, focused document workspace inspired by professional transaction tools. The PDF is visually dominant; warm terracotta accents identify high-value Orqestron actions. Avoid dashboard chrome, decorative cards, and competing panels.

### Design Principles
- Keep the active document stable and central during every workflow change.
- Use progressive disclosure: Forms, AI, and Voice share one contextual right panel.
- Keep AI and Voice visibly available without permanently reducing the PDF canvas.
- Preserve familiar document-editor conventions for close, zoom, save, download, sign, and email.
- Favor dense, precise information hierarchy with accessible focus states and 44px interactive targets.
